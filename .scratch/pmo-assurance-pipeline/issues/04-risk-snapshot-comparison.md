# 04: Risk Snapshot Comparison

**What to build:** A PMO reviewer can compare source-backed risk items across review periods and see which items are new or increased only when their identity is established.

**Blocked by:** 01: Project-Level Budget Assurance.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Each risk snapshot preserves its reporting period, source ID/name, description, status, rating and original scale, owner, mitigation, due date, and WBS when stated.
- [ ] Compare snapshots only when risk IDs match exactly or a reviewer has accepted an identity mapping for the project scope; similar names alone do not prove identity.
- [ ] Do not infer “new” or “increased” without a comparable earlier snapshot and matched identity.
- [ ] Preserve source rating scales; compare ratings across different scales only after an accepted scale mapping.
- [ ] Show item-level risk findings and source citations on the existing Assurance page; do not create a calculated project Risk score or a composite project score.
